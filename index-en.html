HOW TO FIND US?



Ružica Parking Airport, the best choice for you

Save on parking, spend on your journey!

Our affordable private parking lot is located just 3 minutes from Zagreb Airport. We are situated near the old passenger terminal and General Aviation, making us an ideal choice for travelers looking for convenient, safe and affordable parking. Our goal is to build a pleasant, long-term relationship with our customers and provide you with a carefree journey.

FREE TRANSFER

We offer complimentary transportation to and from Zagreb Airport. Our team of friendly and professional staff will ensure a pleasant and welcoming atmosphere.

SAFETY FIRST

Your vehicle will be safe because our parking lot is fenced, illuminated, and under surveillance 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

FLEXIBLE PAYMENT OPTIONS

We accept all types of cards, cash payments, as well as R1 invoices. Payment is made when leaving the parking lot.

SIMPLE PROCESS

Our team ensures that the process of arrival, parking, loading luggage and payment is quick and stress-free.

QUICK TRANSFER AFTER ARRIVAL

After landing at the airport and collecting your luggage, please contact us so that we can pick you up on time.

BOOK IN ADVANCE

Secure your parking spot in our parking lot and rest assured that a space will be waiting for you upon arrival.
